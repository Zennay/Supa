# SUPA future M5 — positive Schema.org candidates require underlying parsed JSON-LD (14 October 2026)

## What the actual M1 inspector can prove
In canonical M1, `inspectCaptureDirectory()` calls `inspectHtml()`, parses JSON-LD and then calls `parseSchemaOrgProduct()`; `schemaOrgProduct: {type:'observation', observation:...}` can arise **only** after a valid JSON-LD parse and creation/validation of a real `RawProductObservation`. A successful capture whose HTML contains **no parsed JSON-LD** or **only failed JSON-LD scripts** cannot make such a Product observation. A `schemaOrgProduct.type='abstain'` record does not contain a positive observation.

The earlier pure offline M5 [product DRAFT #2284](https://github.com/Zennay/Supa/pull/2284), immutable hosted-green `79bbd6afe6a45510db83a5075e8e2ae08ae9522b` (**1,027/1,027 tests PASS**), correctly required own `schemaOrgProduct.type` either `observation` or `abstain`. But a fabricated `{type:'observation'}` **without a real observation**, or a positive decision with no parsed JSON-LD, could still be labeled `candidate_observation` in an otherwise valid-looking future human-only P1/P2/P3 selector queue.
